## He aha a Ayni?

Ko Ayni te whakatinanatanga Solana o tētahi **Whakahaere Mana Motuhake Kore-ingoa (DAO)** — i hangaia tuatahitia hei whakatutuki i ngā hiahia o **AHA (Ancestral Humanity Anonymous)**, engari e tuwhera ana ki ngā whakahaere katoa. I tōna tino tuhinga, he tauira kore-here-mekameka mō ngā kāhui o te ao whānui, i tauira ki te angitu rau-tau o ngā **tikanga AA / 12-takahanga**. Ka hoatu e ia ki tētahi rōpū tētahi huarahi rite-tonu hei **whakauru mema, pupuri pūtea, whakatau mā te hinengaro o te rōpū, me te whakaora mai i ngā kī kua ngaro** — i te wā ka noho **kore-ingoa-noa** ia mema mā ngā tohu mōhio-kore.

- **He aha tāna mahi** — ka whakahaere i tētahi kāhui mematanga i runga i te mekameka: ngā mematanga ā-tau herea-wairua, te pōti kore-ingoa kotahi-mema-kotahi-pōti, te pūtea koha-anake, me tētahi Kaunihera nōhanga-7 me te whakaora kī 4-o-te-7 — kāore he rangatira, kāore he kī whakahaere.
- **Mā wai** — ngā kāhui, ngā rōpū āwhina-tahi, ngā hapori kore-ingoa, ngā DAO e hiahia ana ki te *whai wāhi* kaua ki te whakapae, me te tangata e hiahia ana ki te **tūmataitinga pono** mō ngā mema.
- **Me pēhea te whakamahi** — manatoko i te tauira, whakanoho i tētahi Kaunihera, whakatuwhera i tētahi Porowhita, ā, ka uru mai ngā mema hei tutohinga muna. Tirohia te [Tīmatanga tere](#-tīmatanga-tere).

> *Ayni* (Quechua): te utu tapu o te hoatu me te whiwhi — te tautoko-whaiaro, kotahi mema kotahi reo, kāore he rangatira pokapū.

## ✨ Ngā āhuatanga

- 🪪 **Mematanga herea-wairua** — ā-tau, kore-whakawhiti. Ka riro te whai wāhi mā te mahi me te whakahou, kāore e hokona, kāore e hokona atu.
- 🕶️ **Kore-ingoa-noa** — he tutohinga muna te mema i roto i tētahi huinga Merkle, kaua he pūkoro e kitea ana. He mōhio-kore te pōti me ngā tohu; ka whākina te tuakiri mā te whiriwhiri anake.
- 🗳️ **Pōti hinengaro-rōpū** — kotahi mema, kotahi pōti, ka whakaputaina i te huna (kāore he plutocracy taumaha-tohu), me tētahi whakakāhore ia-kaupapa hei ārai i ngā pōti-rua.
- 🏛️ **Kāore he rangatira, kāore he kī whakahaere** — ko te Kaunihera nōhanga-7 *te* mana, ka mahi anake mā te **4-o-te-7**.
- 🔑 **Whakaora kī e ora ai i te ngaro** — hurihuri i tētahi nōhanga, heke rānei i ia mea o tētahi pūkoro kua ngaro ki tētahi hou, kei muri i tētahi matapaki e taea ana e tētahi nōhanga pono kotahi te whakaoho.
- 🌱 **Porowhita manatoko-taea** — ka whakatū tētahi rōpū i tētahi Porowhita hou me te kāwanatanga ōrite; ka whakakōtui te World Service Circle i ngā rauemi tiri, kāore e whakahaere.
- 📜 **Tohu whāki-ā-tīpako** — ngā tiwhikete akoranga/tīmatatanga ("ko wai, he aha te akoranga, nā wai i whakaako, āhea") e whākina ana *ā-māra ki te māra* i te mōhio-kore.

## 💡 Me pēhea te hono tahi

Pānuihia me he rākau — ko ngā tāngata te tāupoki kei runga, ko ngā Porowhita ngā peka, ā, ko te World Service Circle te tinana me ngā pakiaka kei te pūtake:

```
 member member member      member member member      member member member     ← the members
 (anon) (anon) (anon)      (anon) (anon) (anon)      (anon) (anon) (anon)         (anonymous,
    \      |      /          \      |      /          \      |      /              one member,
     ┌─────┬─────┐            ┌─────┬─────┐            ┌─────┬─────┐               one vote)
     │   Cusco   │            │  Lisbon   │            │  Bangkok  │
     │ Council 7 │            │ Council 7 │            │ Council 7 │   …        ← Circles: the
     └─────┴─────┘            └─────┴─────┘            └─────┴─────┘               branches
           └────────────────────────┬────────────────────────┘
                                    │   every Circle is forked from one shared template
                     ┌───────────────┴───────────────┐
                     │     WORLD SERVICE CIRCLE       │   ← the trunk / root ("foundation"):
                     │   12 Steps · Preamble · docs   │      holds the shared template + a
                     │    7-seat Council · 4-of-7     │      Council (4/7) that coordinates
                     └───────────────────────────────┘      but does NOT govern
```

- **Ngā mema (te tāupoki)** — ka uru ia tangata ki te huinga mema o tētahi Porowhita hei tutohinga kore-ingoa, ka mahi mā ngā tohu; kāore e hiahia ana ki te whakaatu pūkoro. Kotahi mema, kotahi pōti.
- **Ngā Porowhita (ngā peka)** — he rōpū ā-rohe mana motuhake, ia mea me tōna ake Kaunihera nōhanga-7, pūtea, me ngā pōti; i manatokona mai i te tauira.
- **World Service Circle (te tinana/pakiaka)** — ka pupuri i te tauira tiri me ngā tuhinga, ka whakakōtui i te hanganga Porowhita, engari **kāore** e takahi i te hinengaro o te rōpū ā-rohe. He *hononga whakakotahitanga* te hononga, kaua he mekameka whakahau.

## 🎩 Ko wai ka aha — te Kaunihera nōhanga-7

```
                          THE COUNCIL  (7 seats · acts by 4-of-7 · IS the authority)
   ┌──────────────────────── 3 functional servants ────────────────────────┐
   │                                                                        │
   │   TREASURER          SECRETARY            RHYTHM KEEPER                 │
   │   stewards the       admits members,      keeps the cadence of         │
   │   treasury &         records & docs,      meetings/ceremonies;         │
   │   soulbound mint     proposal hygiene     the reference / lead seat    │
   │                                                                        │
   └────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
   ┌──────────────────────── 4 elders of the directions ───────────────────┐
   │                                                                        │
   │   ELDER NORTH      ELDER EAST       ELDER SOUTH       ELDER WEST        │
   │   no daily duty — their role is quorum & resilience, so recovery       │
   │   never depends on the three busy servants alone (medicine wheel)      │
   │                                                                        │
   └────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

   MEMBERS  ──one-member-one-vote (anonymous, ZK)──▶  group-conscience decisions
            the Council serves and recovers; the membership decides direction
```

- **Kāore he kī whakahaere.** Ko ia mahi mana he mahi nōhanga Kaunihera, he marohi 4-o-te-7 rānei.
- **Ka mahi ngā kaimahi, ka whakatau ngā mema.** Ka tukuna ngā mahi noa ki tētahi nōhanga, ā, ka taea te whakakore mā te hurihuri Kaunihera; ka whakatauhia te ahunga mā te pōti a te mema.

## 🚀 Tīmatanga tere

> Ngā mea e hiahiatia ana i mua: **Rust**, **Solana/Agave CLI 2.x**, **Anchor 0.31.1**, **Node 20+**, me (mō ngā tohu) **circom 2.1** + **snarkjs**.

```bash
# 1. clone
git clone https://github.com/Ayniator/Ayni.git && cd Ayni

# 2. install JS deps (proving helpers + tests)
npm install

# 3. build the on-chain program
anchor build

# 4. run the test suite (localnet: memberships, 4-of-7 recovery, a real ZK vote round-trip)
anchor test
```

Koia te oma-tuatahi katoa. Ka whakatūria e ngā whakamātautau tētahi Porowhita, ka tukuna ngā mematanga kore-ingoa, ka oma i tētahi whakaora 4-o-te-7, ā, ka manatika i tētahi tohu pōti Groth16 pono i runga i te mekameka (ā, ka whakakāhore i tētahi whakakāhore kua tukuruatia).

Hei whakatū i tō ake kāhui me te oma i ngā momo pōti e whā, whāia **[IMPLEMENTATION.md](../IMPLEMENTATION.md)** takahanga-mā-takahanga.

## 🤝 Te koha mai

E whakautua ana ngā koha — ngā take, ngā PR, me te arotake o te tuhi-muna inarā.

1. Manatoko, ā, peka mai i `solana` (te peka taunoa; kei `ethereum`, `avalanche` ētahi atu mekameka).
