### Finding 1 — FUNCTIONAL/DESIGN, judged non-critical — self-endorsed faucet grant
**Severity: WARNING (not CRITICAL). Epic: E0/F35.**

Confirmed genuine: `activate_faucet_zk` checks only `wing_peer.active` (an
active bond exists) and a valid `member_vote` tree-membership proof — it never
compares the prover's identity against the neophyte's. `establish_wing_peer`
(`programs/ayni/src/instructions/establish_wing_peer.rs`) is called by the
**mentee**, refuses `mentee == wing`, but does *not* require the *named wing*
to ever act again. Once a neophyte is admitted (`confirm_admission` inserts
their commitment into `MemberTree`) and has designated any real, distinct
member as their wing, they can generate their own `member_vote` proof and
release their own first-gas grant — something the named path structurally
prevented (`activate_faucet` requires the **wing's own signature**).

**Reproduction:** code reading, `programs/ayni/src/instructions/
activate_faucet_zk.rs:151` (only `wing_peer.active` is asserted) vs.
`establish_wing_peer.rs:18` (`mentee != wing`, but wing's future participation
is never re-checked).

**Judgment: not a CRITICAL regression.** It touches none of the seven Layer-D
privacy invariants and no Traditions rule (rank/name-a-sponsor/leak-a-
relationship/aggregate) — if anything it is *more* private than the named path
it partially replaces, since no endorser identity is ever revealed either way.
The economic bound that actually matters is unchanged: one grant per
commitment ever, uniform amount, the neophyte's own wallet, the neophyte's own
Circle's jar, gated by the membership door (two-attestation admission). A
self-served neophyte was always going to receive this same capped, uniform
grant from *someone* — this only changes who presses the button and removes a
ceremonial "someone else must approve" step for a bounded amount of gas money.
**Recommendation, not a blocker:** either accept this trade explicitly in
`BACKLOG.md`/`docs/faucet.md` (it is already disclosed honestly there) and add
a regression test that pins the boundary (self-endorsement succeeds, but only
once, only at the uniform amount, only to one's own wallet), or close it for
real with a new circuit (F44) that binds the endorser to the wing. Currently
**no test pins this boundary either way** — a coverage gap, flagged in
`tests/sentinel/checklist.yaml`.

## Verdict rationale

Everything this round's brief asked Sentinel to check by name held up under
independent re-derivation, not just re-reading the comments that claimed it:
the program builds clean with no stack overflow, the shared grant-payment code
really is the same code in both activation paths, the two anonymous proofs
(admission and faucet) really do produce different nullifiers so they can't be
correlated, the mixing math really is a monotone, non-evicting, sort-before-
cap design, and the member-facing privacy copy neither oversells the
protection nor hides the leak. One genuine capability change was found —
a member can now release their own starter gas without waiting for their
sponsor — and after tracing exactly what it does and does not touch, it is a
loosening of ceremony, not of money or of anyone's privacy, so it is reported
as a warning to fix or formally accept, not a failure. A second, unrelated,
and more serious issue was found and already fixed by another agent mid-round
— every page of the app was quietly telling Google who was visiting, which on
a platform built entirely on anonymity is exactly the kind of thing that
should never happen, and Sentinel independently confirmed the fix actually
closes it rather than just claiming to. The round fails no privacy invariant,
introduces no ranking or naming of members, and hides no baseline change, so
the verdict is PASS — but the paperwork (BACKLOG.md and docs/shipped.md) has
fallen behind the code in two places and a couple of test gaps remain open,
which keeps it at PASS WITH WARNINGS rather than a clean PASS.
