Recommend BACKLOG.md/docs/shipped.md tighten "every page" to "every listed
page" or add settings-security (and `/recovery/setup`, not checked this
round) to a follow-up localisation pass, in a future round — flagging this

gap worth fixing eventually, but it is not something this commit caused or
claimed to fix, so it does not count against this small change. Nothing
else in the file, the build, or the privacy checks moved. Verdict: PASS.
