**2. Shuffle uniformity and non-correlation — read the code, then tested it

`frontend/components/WalletChooser.tsx`'s `shuffle()` is the textbook
**descending** Fisher–Yates: `for (i = a.length-1; i > 0; i--) { j =
Math.floor(Math.random() * (i+1)); swap(a[i], a[j]); }` — correct, unbiased.
The only randomness source is `Math.random()`; there is no seeding by, or
ordering on, wallet address, IP, session, or profile — confirmed by reading
every line that touches `order`/`platform` state. I additionally wrote a
throwaway 200,000-iteration harness (not committed) reimplementing the exact
loop over the 5 real wallet ids and measured the position-frequency
distribution: every wallet lands in every position at 19.82%–20.20% (expected
20.00% flat), **max deviation 1.00%** — comfortably inside a fair-shuffle
bound. No wallet is statistically favoured for any position.

## Verdict rationale

This round reviewed one small, well-scoped change: the "connect a wallet" box
on the Create-a-Circle page used to always suggest Solflare first and Phantom
second — a fixed favourite, which the platform's own rule against
endorsements forbids. The fix swaps that box for the same shuffled,
five-wallet picker already used on the onboarding page, so every visit shows
a different, fair order, and adds one new translated sentence in all
nineteen languages the app supports. I checked all of this by hand and by
machine rather than taking the commit's word for it: I grepped the whole
front end for any wallet name hiding outside the shuffler and found none in
the changed page; I rebuilt and stress-tested the shuffle algorithm myself
200,000 times and it is genuinely fair, not just "correct-looking"; I
confirmed the five wallets and their store links are real, agree between the
two copies of the file, and all still work; I read every one of the nineteen
translations myself and they are real, distinct translations, not
placeholders or copies; and I independently re-ran the whole automated test
suite from a clean state and got the same 161-pass, zero-fail result the
team reported, plus a live browser smoke test of forty-nine pages including
this one. I also independently confirmed the onboarding page the team is
about to mark "done" genuinely exists, works, and is reachable from the
navigation menu — that claim holds up. Nothing in this change touches money,
proofs, or the smart contract, so the deeper privacy checks (nobody can be
tracked, ranked, or have a sponsor exposed) were not re-run from scratch this
round, since nothing in this diff could affect them — but the two changed
files themselves contain no such risk. I did find two small things worth
knowing about that are **not** this commit's fault and do not block it: a
different, older screen still names two wallets by name in a sentence (a
smaller version of the same kind of problem, from months-old code), and both
changed files already had a pre-existing code-formatting complaint that
predates this commit. Both are recorded as warnings so they get fixed later,
not forgotten. Overall: the change does exactly what it claims, breaks
nothing that was working, and does not introduce a new privacy or fairness
problem — **PASS**.
